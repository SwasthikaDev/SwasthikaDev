### About

I run **Calyirex** (also Kautilya GovTech), bootstrapped from India. I build AI tooling for civic and government systems, with a focus on evidence integrity and Section 65B compliant infrastructure. Right now I am piloting the Bridge Device in Jaipur and shipping a CCTV evidence pipeline that real officers can actually use.

### What I am working on

- **Bridge Device**, a hardware unit for forensic grade evidence capture
- A **face matching copilot** for law enforcement workflows
- The boring kind of civic AI, the kind that actually ships into government desks
